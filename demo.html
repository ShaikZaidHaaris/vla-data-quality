Expected format

One row per timestep, with a header line:

episode,step,eef_x,eef_y,eef_z,gripper,success

episode groups rows into demonstrations.
eef_x/y/z is the gripper position in metres.
gripper is the command, positive means closing.
success is 1 if that demonstration finished the task.
